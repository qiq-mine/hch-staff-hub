# Role Avatar Prompt Engineering Masterclass

Use this prompt formula to generate consistent digital employee and role avatars for any department or organization.

---

## 1. Master Formula Template

```text
Minimalist flat line-art avatar portrait of a {gender} {role_name} in the exact art style of Japanese editorial minimalist flat line illustration (Noritake and Yu Nagaba style). Thick clean black ink contour lines, simple black oval bead eyes, calm and approachable expression, small hook nose, ear with a small plus sign detail, flat gray triangular shadow under the jawline{blush_clause}. {hair_style} with soft {hair_color} accent color, wearing {clothing_description}. In the background behind {pronoun} shoulder, a minimalist floating card showing {role_specific_prop}. Off-white plain solid background, flat 2D vector graphic look, high aesthetic quality, clean portrait bust, centered composition.
```
