# Role Avatar Style Guide (Minimalist Line Art)

## 1. Aesthetic Origin & Art Direction

This illustration system is inspired by contemporary Japanese editorial flat line art (notably artists like **Noritake**, **Yu Nagaba**, and **Face Oka**). It conveys minimalism, clarity, human warmth, and modern tech sophistication.

---

## 2. Core Visual Elements (DNA)

| Anatomy Feature | Design Rules | Hex / Visual Spec |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linework (Outlines)** | Continuous, smooth, clean black ink strokes with uniform thickness; rounded stroke terminals. | `#1A1A1A` or `#000000` |
| **Eyes** | Solid black oval beads/dots; no complex iris or specular highlights. | High-contrast black bead |
| **Nose** | A single minimalist hook or angular contour line. | Minimalist hook |
| **Mouth** | A single subtle curved line or slightly open crescent expressing relaxed confidence. | Clean single stroke |
| **Ear Detail** | Outer rounded ear contour with an iconic internal **plus sign (`+`)** or minimal hook representing the inner ear. | Iconic `+` inner ear |
| **Jaw Shadow** | A flat, geometric triangular gray shadow cast directly under the jawline onto the neck. | `#D1D5DB` or `#9CA3AF` flat fill |
| **Cheek Blush** | Subtle soft circular blush mark on the cheek (optional, used for warm/approachable roles). | Soft pastel pink/coral (`#FBCFE8` / `#F87171` at low opacity) |
| **Background** | Clean, flat, distraction-free neutral off-white background. | `#F9FAFB` or `#FFFFFF` |

---

## 4. Role Identifier Prop Guidelines

* **Placement**: Floated subtly behind the subject's right or left shoulder.
* **Style**: Clean rounded-rectangle card (`border-radius: 12px`, thin black border `#1A1A1A`).
* **Content**: 2D minimalist vector glyphs, simplified bar charts, color tag blocks, or wireframes.
* **Depth**: Flat, zero drop shadows, maintaining 2D editorial purity.
